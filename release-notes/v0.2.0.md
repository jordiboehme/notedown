# A picture is worth a thousand tokens.

A new OCR that looks at drawings first, and a library that shows your notes before the OCR is done.

## New & Noteworthy

### A new OCR

- Notedown now reads handwriting with Qwen3.5-4B. Two small PaddlePaddle models find the drawings and the lines of text on a page first. All three models are Apache-2.0.
- Text is read from the whole page with the drawings left out. Handwriting comes out more exact than before, and printed text too.
- Each drawing is read as a diagram or as a sketch. A diagram becomes a Mermaid chart. A sketch gets a short description and is never turned into a chart.
- Diagrams are a bit less exact than before: on our test pages 14 of 18 arrows are right, where the old OCR had 17.
- Pages with only text are faster than before. Pages with diagrams take longer, about 30 seconds on a Mac with an M5.
- The model download is about 3.6 GB. Notedown shows the licences of the four files before it starts.
- The new OCR is made for handwriting. Printed diagrams are not a focus of this version.

### The library

- A note shows all its pages at once, also before the OCR has read it. Each page without text says "Waiting for the OCR."
- The paper is always white, also in dark mode.
- The pages can be zoomed (Cmd+Plus, Cmd+Minus and Cmd+0), and the divider between pages and text can be dragged. A double click opens one page in a large view.
- Search finds notes and files by name and by the words in their text (Cmd+F). A click on a text hit opens the note at that place.
- Save Markdown saves the text of the open note anywhere (Cmd+Shift+S).
- Mark All as Seen clears the New count in one step.

### Pause the OCR

- The menu bar panel and its menu can pause the OCR for 1 minute, 5 minutes, 1 hour or until you resume it. A pause stops the OCR at once and frees the memory of the model. The sync with the Supernote keeps running. New Markdown files and deletes in the data folder wait until the pause ends.

### Licence

- The OCR code of Notedown (the notedown-ocr crate) is now MPL-2.0, so it can be shared with Rezzer. Notedown itself stays AGPL-3.0-or-later. Released versions keep their licence.

## Breaking changes

- The OCR needs new model files. The app asks for the download. On the command line, run `notedown model pull` after the update.
- Pages the old OCR has read keep their text on the server until they change. `notedown convert` reads such pages again on its next run.
- The old model files (3.4 GB) stay on disk until you remove them. The OCR page in Settings offers it, and `notedown model clean-old` does the same.
